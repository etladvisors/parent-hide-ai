# Privacy Policy for Parent Hide AI

**Last updated:** August 2026

Parent Hide AI is a parental control extension. It does not transmit any personal data or browsing data off the device. It does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind. No data is shared with third parties.

The extension does two things:

1. **AI content filtering.** It modifies the appearance of Google Search result pages by hiding AI-generated content elements, and redirects certain Google URLs (AI Mode) to standard search results.
2. **Search keyword filtering.** It blocks searches on supported search and social sites when the search query matches a keyword list configured by a parent, and blocks a parent-configured list of websites. When a search or site is blocked, the extension may record the blocked query or domain **locally on the device** (in the browser's extension storage) so a parent can review it from the extension's options page. The extension may also record searches performed on the supported sites **locally on the device** for the same parental-review purpose. These logs never leave the device via the extension, are capped at a fixed number of entries, and can be disabled or cleared at any time.

**Blocklist updates.** The extension may periodically download its keyword and site blocklist (configuration data only — never code) from a parent-managed server, so a parent can update the filter without reinstalling the extension. This request sends no personal data, no browsing data, and no search queries — it is a plain download of the parent's current blocklist. If the download fails, the extension keeps using its last known list.

All filtering and matching occurs locally on the user's device.
